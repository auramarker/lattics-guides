When managing content, it is necessary to move content to different projects or interfaces. Lattics provides several very convenient drag and drop functions:

1. Drag and drop from the project outline, card library, search results to <Highlight color="Yellow">the editor or graph</Highlight>

   <Video src="/images/dragtoarticle_en.mp4" />

   Drag and drop the contents into the editor, they will automatically become title citations; drag and drop them into the graph, they will become a node.

2. Drag and drop from <Highlight color="Yellow">the graph</Highlight> to the project outline or editor

   <Video src="/images/graphToTree_en.mp4" />

   Drag and drop a node in the graph to the project outline, it will become an article in the outline; dragging it into the editor will turn it into a title citation.

3. Drag and drop the paragraphs in <Highlight color="Yellow">the editor</Highlight> to the card library or the graph

   Drag and drop <Highlight color="Yellow">a text block</Highlight> in the editor to the card library, it will become a card; drag it to the graph, it will become a node. At the same time, blocks in the text become full-text citations.

4. Drag and drop from the card library to the project outline

   <Video src="/images/cardtotree_en.mp4" />

   Drag and drop a card in the card library to the project outline, it can be converted into an article. On the contrary, drag and drop an article in the project outline into the card library, it can be converted into a card.

5. Drag and drop from one project outline to another <Highlight color="Yellow">project outline</Highlight>

   <Video src="/images/treeToTree_en.mp4" />

   Dragging an article from one project's outline to another project's outline will move the article too.

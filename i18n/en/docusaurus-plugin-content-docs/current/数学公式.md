In order to facilitate the writing of research papers and technical articles of science and engineering, Lattics has carefully designed and developed a mathematical formula editor that integrates elegant and simple style.

<Video src="/images/math_en.mp4" />

Tip: Math formulas are in the <Highlight color="Yellow">/ menu</Highlight>

Lattics' mathematical formulas support most commonly used <Highlight color="Yellow">algebraic operators</Highlight>, <Highlight color="Yellow">Greek letters</Highlight>, <Highlight color="Yellow">sets</Highlight>, <Highlight color="Yellow">functions</Highlight>, <Highlight color="Yellow">geometry</Highlight>, <Highlight color="Yellow">trigonometric functions</Highlight>, <Highlight color="Yellow">calculus</Highlight>, etc., and can meet most of requirements. <Highlight color="Yellow">LaTeX syntax</Highlight> is also supported.

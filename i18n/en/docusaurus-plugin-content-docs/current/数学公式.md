In order to facilitate the writing of academic papers, technical articles on science, or engineering technology articles, Lattics has specially designed and developed a math equation editor that combines beauty and ease of use.

There are two ways to create math equation. One is through the options in the / menu, and the other is to use the $ $ syntax. Note that this syntax requires spaces between $$. Lattics supports inline math equations, that is it can be added between text, such as$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f\left(t\right)e^{-iwt}dt$

Lattics' math equation editor support most commonly used algebraic operators, Greek letters, sets, functions, geometry, trigonometric functions, calculus, etc., which can meet most formula needs.

<Image src="/images/math/No-765-image.png" width={80}></Image>


Click "LaTeX Syntax" in the lower left corner of the math equation editor to directly enter LaTeX syntax. When entering LaTeX math formulas, Lattics also provides a very considerate syntax completion function.

<Image src="/images/math/No-1044-image.png" width={80}></Image>


In the style template, you can also set the font size, color, and alignment for math equation to meet various layout requirements.

Note: These styles are only effective for math equations in independent paragraphs, and will not be affected by inline math equations. The height of inline math equation will follow the height and color of the paragraph text, which is 120% of the font height and has the same color as font color

After collecting and preparing a lot of inspiration, you can start to build the outline. Lattics provides two tools to assist in the conception of the outline:

1. <Highlight color="Yellow">Mind Map</Highlight>

   <Video src="/images/mindnodeadd_en.mp4" />

   Through mind mapping, you can intuitively conceive the outline and adjust it easily. Such as adding, deleting, moving a node, modifying the title, etc., you can also use different colors to mark the nodes.

2. <Highlight color="Yellow">Project Outline</Highlight>

   <Video src="/images/hierarchy_en.mp4" />

   The project outline is great for adjusting the writing outline in a linear narrative way, because it has been structured exactly like the final draft from top-down and divided into chapters.

   Note: You can also select multiple articles to merge in the project outline, which is not available in mind maps.

After all the writing is done, it's time to fine layout the content. Lattics provides some typesetting tools:

1. Style Settings

   <Video src="/images/styleSetting_en.mp4" />

   Select a piece of text, and there will be many settings in the right-click menu, including font, size, color, highlight color, alignment, bold and so on. For details, please refer to the introduction of the previous "Style Settings".

2. Convert Paragraph Style

   <Video src="/images/convert_en.mp4" />

   The paragraph has a variety of styles, such as headings, body text, lists, quotations, and so on. Lattics provides a way to convert paragraphs of text between different styles. For example, you can turn the body into a title, a list into the body, and so on.

   Click the action icon in the upper left corner of the paragraph, and there will be an option of "Turn to..." at the top of the menu.

3. Multi-column layout of images and text

   <Video src="/images/duoColumn_en.mp4" />

   Lattics supports 3-column layout. By dragging blocks such as paragraphs and images, you can easily complete multi-column layout, and you can also adjust the width of each column. For details, please refer to the introduction of "Multi-column Layout of Images and Text".

4. Adjust the Image

   <Video src="/images/imagescale_en.mp4" />

   In the text, you can drag and drop the four corners of the image to resize the image, or you can add a title at the bottom of the image. In the future, functions such as cropping and zooming in pictures will also be supported, so stay tuned...

5. Style Template

   <Video src="/images/template_en.mp4" />

   Style templates are very powerful and useful. If you have your own article style, you can customize the style for each block of the article, and then save it as a template. For details, please refer to the introduction of the previous "Style Template".

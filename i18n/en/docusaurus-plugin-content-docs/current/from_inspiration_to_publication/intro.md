Lattics designs follow the principles of the brain and serve the creation. Whether people are writing, thinking, learning, researching, summarizing, etc., all belong to a kind of creative activity. The law of creative activities is to start from vague, divergent, fragmented information and ideas, and gradually form an orderly structure. Therefore, in essence, creation is the decrease of entropy, turning disorder into order.

Next, we'll walk through the process of using Lattics from ideation to publication. Of course, this process does not need to start from 1 to 10. It can be get started from any point. There is no fixed sequence. The order here is just for the convenience of the overall and orderly introduction of Lattics.

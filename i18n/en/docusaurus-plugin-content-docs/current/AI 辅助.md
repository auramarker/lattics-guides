## AI Assistance

As large language models continue to evolve, AI has become useful in content generation, data analysis, software development, and research workflows. Lattics AI features are designed to support writing, deep research, and ideation while keeping model choice, human review, batch processing, and privacy protection under user control.

### 1. Multiple Model Providers

Lattics supports mainstream AI model integration methods. You can connect providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, GLM, and Kimi, use third-party model access services such as OpenRouter and OpenCode, connect local Ollama models, or use models deployed in your own cloud environment.

<Image src="/images/ai/model-providers.jpg" width={80}></Image>

### 2. Model Settings for Each Tool

AI translation, proofreading, rewriting, creative writing, and deep research can each use a separately assigned model. This lets you choose different models for different tasks, such as one model for translation and another for research or creative writing.

<Image src="/images/ai/tool-model-settings.jpg" width={80}></Image>

### 3. Human and AI Collaboration

Lattics lets you keep editing AI-generated results. For example, if an AI translation is not good enough, you can revise the translated text directly. Your revision can then be used as a reference and correction for future translation work.

<Image src="/images/ai/editable-translation.jpg" width={80}></Image>

You can also comment on AI-generated results and revisit previous AI versions. If a previous rewrite is better after several rounds of changes, you can return to that version and continue improving it.

<Video src="/images/ai/version-history.mp4" width={80}></Video>

In AI Chat, you can drag content directly into Lattics articles. When the content includes web links, Lattics keeps those links and turns them into reference information with title, URL, and access date, which is useful for academic writing and source management.

<Video src="/images/ai/ai-citations.mp4" width={80}></Video>

### 4. Batch Processing

You can select multiple articles in the project outline for batch translation or rewriting. You can also @ mention multiple articles in AI Chat and ask AI to process them together, improving the efficiency of research organization and content refinement.

<Image src="/images/ai/batch-at-mentions.jpg" width={80}></Image>

### 5. Privacy Protection

Lattics provides project-scoped AI access control. By default, AI can only read selected text, specified articles, or content explicitly referenced with @. It cannot access all project content automatically. If you want to use project content as a local knowledge base, you need to grant AI access to the corresponding project first.

To further protect sensitive data, Lattics anonymizes content submitted to AI, including phone numbers, email addresses, ID card numbers, API keys, tokens, and other private information.

With advances in AI technology, small local models can now synthesize fairly natural speech. The Lattics team has applied this technology to reading articles aloud and made it available to everyone for free.

There is a voice icon in the bottom-right corner of the editor. Click it to open the AI Read Aloud interface.

### Download the Model

Before using AI Read Aloud for the first time, you need to download the AI speech synthesis model. Click the "Download Model" button in the AI Read Aloud interface, and the app will automatically download the model and complete the setup.

### Switch Voices

Lattics includes a variety of voice files for English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and other languages. While AI Read Aloud is running, you can manually select the voice you want to use. If you have added an AI Provider, AI Read Aloud will also automatically analyze the style of the article and select a suitable voice.

### Upgrade the Model

As AI technology advances rapidly, the AI speech synthesis model will also receive updates and upgrades. When an upgrade is available, a notification will appear in the AI Read Aloud interface. Click "Upgrade Model" to upgrade the model automatically, with no further steps required.

### Delete the Model

The AI speech synthesis model takes up approximately 2–3 GB of storage. If you no longer use AI Read Aloud, click the more options button in the top-right corner of the AI Read Aloud interface and select "Delete Model" to remove the AI speech synthesis model and free up the storage space it occupies.

### Data Security + Free to Use

The AI speech synthesis model is downloaded to your laptop or desktop computer and runs locally, so your article content is not uploaded to any server. This provides a high level of data security, and the feature is free to use.

**Note:**

The AI Read Aloud speech synthesis model requires substantial memory and GPU computing resources while running, so your laptop or desktop computer must meet certain hardware requirements. To ensure a good experience, this feature will not be available on computers with lower specifications.

The minimum device requirements for AI Read Aloud are Windows 11 24H2 or later, or macOS 15 or later, with at least 8 GB of memory.

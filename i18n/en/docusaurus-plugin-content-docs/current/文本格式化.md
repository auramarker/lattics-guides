title: Text formatting
---
Lattics provides up to 13 rules for text formatting. We divide them into three groups, from top to bottom, including removing or retaining blank lines between paragraphs, processing spaces in paragraphs, and processing special symbols, such as capitalizing the first letter, converting straight quote mark to curly quote mark, converting full-width punctuation to half-width punctuation, and automatically completing matching punctuation, etc.

You can enable or disable these rules as needed, and then click "Run" to format the text content in the article with one click.

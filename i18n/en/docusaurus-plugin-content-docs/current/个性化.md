Lattics offers a variety of customized capabilities:

1. <Highlight color="Yellow">Customized Icons</Highlight>

   <Video src="/images/icon_en.mp4" />

   Project icons and article icons in the project outline can be replaced with emoji to display the distinction between projects and articles.

2. <Highlight color="Yellow">Themes</Highlight>

   <Video src="/images/theme_en.mp4" />

   Lattics provides two sets of themes, day and night, and other themes will be launched in the near future.

3. <Highlight color="Yellow">Preferred font and the size</Highlight>

   <Image src="/images/preference_en.png" />

   In the editor preferences, you can set the font and the size of the text, which apply for all articles (unless the article is specified to use a special style template).

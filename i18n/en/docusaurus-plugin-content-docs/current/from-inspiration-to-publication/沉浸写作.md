1. Focus Mode

   When devoting yourself to writing, you may want to hide other irrelevant interfaces and content, and focus only on the editing. So, Lattics will provide focus mode. After turning it on, the editor will be displayed in full screen, and other interfaces, such as the project list, project outline, card library, etc., will be hidden, so as not to interfere with writing.

2. Sequence Reading

   <Video src="/images/continue.mp4" />

   After you've written a section of content, you can enable sequential reading in your project outline to chain together chapters you've already written and read them through to see the details of the transitions between chapters.

   **Tips**\: In sequence reading mode, WYSIWYG(What You See Is What You Get) editing is also possible.
